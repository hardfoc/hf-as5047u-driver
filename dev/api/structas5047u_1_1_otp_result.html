Detailed Description

Outcome of AS5047U::ProgramOTP().

The step numbers are those of the datasheet's step-by-step OTP procedure (DS000637 v1-01, page 41-42).

◆ burn_started

bool as5047u::OtpResult::burn_started = false

True once step 13 (PROGOTP) was sent: the fuses may be burned, even if a later step failed. A part that failed after this must not be used.
